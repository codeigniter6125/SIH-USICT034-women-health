Refine the CURRENT She Care login screen rather than redesigning it from scratch.

The existing design has a good foundation: clean mobile-first layout, warm neutral background, teal healthcare accent, OTP authentication, Maya introduction, and privacy messaging.

Upgrade it to feel like a premium, production-ready healthcare product suitable for a national-level hackathon.

IMPORTANT:
Preserve the existing functionality and OTP login flow.
Do NOT add unnecessary fields.
Do NOT redesign the entire application.
Improve hierarchy, branding, spacing, typography, accessibility, and emotional connection.

## 1. BRAND IDENTITY

Strengthen the She Care brand.

Replace the current location-pin-like logo with a simple, distinctive healthcare/wellbeing symbol that communicates:

* care
* trust
* women's health
* companionship
* safety

Do NOT use:

* location pins
* medical crosses as the primary logo
* excessive hearts
* flowers
* stereotypical feminine symbols

Keep the logo minimal and recognizable at small sizes.

The brand should feel modern and trustworthy rather than decorative.

## 2. MAYA BRANDING

Make Maya more memorable.

The current "Guided by Maya, your health companion" pill is a good concept, but elevate it into a stronger brand element.

Create:

* a small recognizable Maya avatar
* subtle visual identity
* clear "Maya" typography
* supportive but professional appearance

Maya should feel like a calm AI companion, NOT a doctor.

Suggested copy:

"Guided by Maya"
"Your health companion"

Keep the wording concise.

## 3. HERO HIERARCHY

Improve the top section.

Use:

She Care

"Your private space for understanding
and tracking your health."

Then:

"Guided by Maya"
"Your health companion"

The brand name should be the strongest visual element.
The supporting sentence should be clearly readable but secondary.

## 4. BILINGUAL ACCESSIBILITY

Introduce subtle Hindi + English support without clutter.

Use:

"नमस्ते 👋"

or a similarly subtle bilingual greeting above the main authentication card.

Do NOT translate every piece of UI text on this screen.

Instead, establish that She Care is designed for both Hindi and English users.

Add a small language control:

English | हिन्दी

Make it accessible but unobtrusive.

## 5. LOGIN CARD

Keep the OTP login card.

Improve it visually with:

* slightly stronger hierarchy
* refined border
* subtle shadow
* comfortable internal spacing
* larger touch-friendly controls
* clear field label
* clear CTA

Heading:

"Sign in or create account"

Supporting text:

"Enter your mobile number to get started"

Mobile number input:

"+91   98765 43210"

Primary CTA:

"Send OTP"

The CTA should be the strongest action on the screen.

## 6. PHONE INPUT

Make the phone input feel premium.

Use a clear country selector:

🇮🇳 +91

Then the phone number field.

Ensure:

* large touch target
* clear focus state
* readable placeholder
* no tiny text

## 7. PRIVACY MESSAGE

The current privacy section is useful but too small.

Make it slightly more readable.

Use a small privacy/security icon.

Copy:

"Your health data is private and encrypted."

Then:

"She Care never sells or shares your information without your permission."

Keep this concise.

Do not make the privacy paragraph visually dominant.

## 8. LEGAL TEXT

Keep Terms and Privacy Policy at the bottom.

Increase readability slightly while keeping it secondary.

Use:

"By continuing, you agree to our Terms & Privacy Policy."

Do not make this extremely tiny.

## 9. COLOR SYSTEM

Preserve the current warm neutral + teal direction.

Refine it into a consistent She Care palette:

* warm off-white background
* deep charcoal text
* calm teal primary
* soft teal secondary surfaces
* muted warm neutrals
* emergency red reserved ONLY for safety-critical UI

Do NOT make the application predominantly pink.

Do NOT introduce neon colors.

Do NOT use excessive gradients.

## 10. REMOVE DISTRACTIONS

Remove the pink outline/frame around the phone mockup if it is part of the actual product UI.

The actual application should not depend on a decorative phone frame.

Focus attention on the interface itself.

## 11. SPACING

Reduce excessive empty vertical space.

The screen should feel:

* spacious
* breathable
* intentional

but not empty.

Create a clear vertical rhythm:

Logo
↓
Brand
↓
Maya identity
↓
Login card
↓
Privacy
↓
Legal

Keep consistent spacing between sections.

## 12. ACCESSIBILITY

Ensure:

* minimum 44px touch targets
* readable body text
* strong text/background contrast
* visible input focus states
* icons never communicate information without text
* important actions are clearly labeled

Design for women who may have low digital literacy.

## 13. EMOTIONAL DESIGN

The emotional message of the screen should be:

"You are safe here."
"You can understand your health here."
"Maya is here to help."

But communicate this through visual hierarchy rather than adding lots of marketing copy.

Avoid making the screen sentimental or overly feminine.

## 14. FINAL QUALITY BAR

The final result should look like:

Premium healthcare startup
+
AI companion
+
India-first accessibility
+
Trustworthy medical UX

It should NOT look like:

Generic AI dashboard
Generic hospital portal
Generic women's period tracker
Generic Figma template

Keep the design elegant, minimal, warm, trustworthy and highly usable.

Preserve the current successful parts of the design while making the above refinements.
